Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does pest treatment cost?

It depends on the pest, the size of the property, and how far the infestation has spread. We inspect first, give you a written price before anything starts, and you decide from there.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. The initial consultation and estimate are free.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — registered with the California Structural Pest Control Board under #PR 6008, licensed and insured.

Are treatments safe for kids and pets?

We use licensed, label-approved products and tell you exactly what's applied, where, and how long to stay out of the treated area before it's safe to return.

What pests do you treat?

Ants, cockroaches, spiders, fleas, bed bugs, rodents, termites, bees and wasps, and most other common household and commercial pests in Southern California.

What areas do you serve?

Long Beach and the surrounding South Bay and Southeast Los Angeles County cities, including Signal Hill, Lakewood, Carson, Bellflower, and Paramount.
